Independent interpretations: three views, four methods

Twelve routes began from original rasters with no intermediate geometry or evaluator feedback shared between methods. All saved native files passed persistence checks. One Robust candidate was corrected and reevaluated. Internal scores were too generous on some mechanically wrong nut contours.

Neka and Robust MCP

Both passed 3/3 trials on the same 140-entity model. Median time including startup, rendering and cleanup: Neka 23.33 s, Robust 30.39 s.

This compares execution, not model interpretation. Six trials are too few to establish reliability.

Where the QCAD campaign spent its time
