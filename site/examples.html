Examples

Two successful native CAD operations, with downloadable files.

These examples use synthetic geometry. They show editable output and saved-file checks; they are not scan-reconstruction accuracy tests.

FreeCAD: change a dimension

Increase a constrained rectangle from 100 to 120 units wide. Keep its height and both circle radii unchanged; preserve the circles' relationships to the edges.

Download FreeCAD output

QCAD: hatch a material region

Create a native ANSI31 hatch inside a closed contour, excluding the rectangular hole. Put the hatch and outline on separate layers.

Download input JSON   Download DXF output

From these operations to drawing conversion

The agent builds a larger drawing from validated components and checks the result. Experiments have produced editable drafts from degraded scans. Those customer drawings and derived images are not part of the public release.
